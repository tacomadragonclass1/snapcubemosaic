Snap Cube Mosaic Generator

Turn a picture into a buildable snap-cube design. Everything runs inside this browser — no image is ever uploaded anywhere.

1 Start a design

Flat-colour pixel art works best. Transparent areas become empty cells.

2 Edit the design

Pick a colour, then click or drag across the grid to paint. Right-click a cube to remove it. Number keys 1–0 pick colours, E picks the eraser, Ctrl+Z undoes.
